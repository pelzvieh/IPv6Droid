<p>To actually use this app, a tunnel service is required. You can run your own tunnel service on
suitable infrastructure. See <a href="https://github.com/pelzvieh/ipv6server/">IPv6Server</a>
for more details.</p>

<p>Notes:</p>
<ol>
  <li>This approach towards IPv6 has subtle implications on your privacy: for IPv6 connections,
      you have a fixed IP and you - as a person! - are easily identifyable by all IPv6 communication
      partners. Also, your mobile device suddenly has an IP on which it is reachable from the
      outside.
  </li>
  <li>This app cannot be used when another VPN is active.</li>
  <li>It is not required to have a rooted device.</li>
</ol>
